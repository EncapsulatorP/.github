## The strongest results, ranked

Ranked by three things: the statement is exact, the proof is written down, and it opens a concrete next step.

| # | Result | What it says | Label | Read |
|:-:|---|---|:-:|---|
| 1 | **The sharp tower envelope** | The upper phase of a sign-flipping tower has an exact global maximum, `v_max = exp(W₀(1/e)) ≈ 1.3211`, reached at base `c ≈ 2.1318`. Every smaller excess above 1 occurs at exactly two bases. | Exact | [thread](https://encapsulatorp.github.io/#atlas?entry=tower-envelope) · [proof](https://github.com/EncapsulatorP/EncapsulatorP.github.io/blob/main/atlas/mathematical-notes.md#sharp-tower-envelope) |
| 2 | **Two phases, one contraction** | For every base `c > 1` the alternating tower `u = c^(−v), v = c^u` has a unique pair of phases, with one uniform bound `|F′| ≤ 4·e^(1/e−2) ≈ 0.782 < 1`. | Exact | [thread](https://encapsulatorp.github.io/#atlas?entry=alternating-tower-convergence) · [proof](https://github.com/EncapsulatorP/EncapsulatorP.github.io/blob/main/atlas/mathematical-notes.md#alternating-real-towers) |
| 3 | **The order of prime layers** | Swap two prime-indexed layers around `Ω = W(1)`: the slope is shared, the curvature is not. The difference is `(Ω²/4)·ln p·ln q·ln(q/p)·u²` to leading order. | Exact | [thread](https://encapsulatorp.github.io/#atlas?entry=prime-layer-waves) · [derivation](https://github.com/EncapsulatorP/EncapsulatorP.github.io/blob/main/atlas/recovered-bridges.md#omega-layer-order) |
| 4 | **The logistic quarter-turn** | At `r* = 1 + √6`, where the logistic two-cycle period-doubles, its paired response matrix satisfies `C² = −I`. | Exact | [thread](https://encapsulatorp.github.io/#atlas?entry=logistic-quarter-turn) · [derivation](https://github.com/EncapsulatorP/EncapsulatorP.github.io/blob/main/atlas/recovered-bridges.md#logistic-quarter-turn) |
| 5 | **The Goldbach mirror** | Centre and half-gap coordinates give `pq = m² − d²`; one prime polynomial counts Goldbach pairs by reflection and twin primes by translation; the mirror ratio satisfies `ρ(N) < ½` exactly when `N` has a prime pair. | Exact identities | [thread](https://encapsulatorp.github.io/#atlas?entry=prime-polynomial) · [lemmas](https://github.com/EncapsulatorP/EncapsulatorP.github.io/blob/main/lemmas/LEMMAS.md) · [play](https://encapsulatorp.github.io/interactive/goldbach-involution.html) |
| 6 | **Catalan's constant at the centre** | The map `w = (s−2)/(s+1)` puts `β(2) = G` at the centre of a disk whose boundary is the critical line of the Dirichlet beta function. | Exact | [thread](https://encapsulatorp.github.io/#atlas?entry=beta-catalan-disk) · [derivation](https://github.com/EncapsulatorP/EncapsulatorP.github.io/blob/main/atlas/recovered-bridges.md#beta-disk) |
| 7 | **Collatz meets Kesten–Goldie** | In the 7x+1 run, three estimates of the tail exponent land within 1% of each other: 0.3429 (moment root), 0.3447 (sample moments), 0.3417 (tail fit). | Reported | [model and proofs](https://encapsulatorp.github.io/#kesten-goldie) · [repo](https://github.com/EncapsulatorP/collatz_kesten-goldie) |
| 8 | **The Abel orbit signature** | A discounted reciprocal orbit has an exact limiting signature on cycles. The Collatz cycle (1, 4, 2) leaves `7/12`. | Exact | [thread](https://encapsulatorp.github.io/#atlas?entry=abel-orbit-signature) · [derivation](https://github.com/EncapsulatorP/EncapsulatorP.github.io/blob/main/atlas/recovered-bridges.md#abel-orbit-signature) |

**A result we are proud of for a different reason.** An early tower value sat within 0.0003 of the Brun deficit `2 − B₂`. Result 1 then proved that such a match can be tuned by the base. The program explained its own coincidence, and the fixed-target test that follows from it is next step 4 below.

*Results 1 to 4 and 8 are constructions built inside this program. A systematic literature check has not been run yet; where a result was already known, the site credits the earlier authors.*

## How to read the labels

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Exact** | Proved, with the argument written down. Lean formalization is pending unless stated. |
| **Finite computation** | Checked on a stated finite range. Says nothing beyond that range. |
| **Reported** | Recovered from a saved run. Reproduction is a listed next step. |
| **Conjectural** | A hypothesis under test. |
| **Exploratory** | A construction or picture without a claim yet. |
| **Tooling** | Research infrastructure. |

Repositories are experiments, not automatically mathematical claims.
